# Check your numbers

1. **Plausibility:** is the number in a believable range for the period (not negative, not more than the orders or
   trips that exist in it)?
2. **A second view when it is cheap:** a total by day should add up to the total for the week; a count of trips should
   not exceed the vehicles times the days.
3. **If two views disagree,** report both and say which you trust and why. Never pick one silently.
4. **Say what was not counted** (cancelled orders, open orders, missing distances).
