Recommendation: no pivot to the Lab.

- **Direction: Anubhavam**, a self-improving multilingual agent over business data that learns from its own history and gives each new customer of the same type a domain pack. Blueprint: https://claude.ai/artifact/QFNR4DDu6DQgnB94cK7ZAj

A second company (B) went **0% → 100%** on regional tasks using A's domain pack, with **0 leaks**. Muse's own privacy labels were wrong 3 times, but the rule-based filter was right. Two double-booking slips appeared only in knowledge arms: watch this.
